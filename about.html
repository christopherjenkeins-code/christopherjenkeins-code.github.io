Our Design Approach

Every major decision on this site is grounded in research on what actually gets hospitality customers through the door, rather than guesswork:

  • The layout is mobile-first and responsive because the large majority of diners research a restaurant on their phone before visiting, and most will not trust a site that isn't mobile-friendly (Sauce, 2025).
  • We lead with large, high-quality photography of our food and drinks on the Home and Menu pages, since content built around strong imagery consistently drives far higher engagement than text alone (MenuTiger, 2026).
  • Professional, dish-accurate food photography was prioritised over stock imagery, as it is consistently identified as the single highest-impact design element on a restaurant website (RichMenu, 2026).
  • Navigation was kept to five clear, single-click destinations (Home, About, Menu, Services, Contact) because most diners decide whether to visit or order based directly on what they find on the website itself (ChowNow, 2026).
  • All meaningful images include descriptive alt text, in line with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines, so the site remains usable for visitors relying on screen readers (W3C, 2023).
